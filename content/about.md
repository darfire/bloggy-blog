**Peri pragmaton** - περὶ πραγμάτων - is Ancient Greek for "about things", or
"concerning matters".

> Ταράσσει τοὺς ἀνθρώπους οὐ τὰ πράγματα, ἀλλὰ τὰ περὶ τῶν πραγμάτων
> δόγματα.
>
> Tarassei tous anthrōpous ou ta pragmata, alla ta peri tōn pragmatōn
> dogmata.
>
> Men are disturbed not by things, but by their opinions about things.
>
> — Epictetus, **Enchiridion** 5
